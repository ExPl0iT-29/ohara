## Reddit (r/androidapps first; check each subreddit's self-promotion rules)

**Title:** I built a free read-later app that keeps everything on your phone (no account, no ads)

**Body:**
> When Pocket shut down I wanted somewhere to put the articles I keep meaning to read, without signing up for another service. So I built Ohara.
>
> - Share a link from any app (or paste a batch) and it turns into a clean reader page
> - Works offline once saved, remembers where you stopped
> - Tags, search across full text, quotes with notes, archive
> - Everything is stored on the phone. No account, no ads, no analytics, no AI. Export the whole library to a file any time.
>
> What it doesn't do yet: sync between devices, iOS, or reading pages behind a login.
>
> It's free: PLAY_LINK
> Source: https://github.com/ExPl0iT-29/ohara
>
> I'd love feedback, especially sites that don't extract well.

Reply to every comment in the first two hours. Don't cross-post the same text the same day.
